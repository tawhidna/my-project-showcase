# GarmentsPro – Project Overview

## 1. Project Introduction

GarmentsPro is a Visual Basic 6.0 desktop application designed to support business operations and management information requirements in the garment manufacturing industry.

The application brings together information relating to capacity planning, sample planning, export order booking, order allocation, production follow-up, sewing and cutting status, shipment information, and management reporting.

The project presentation demonstrates a range of operational screens and report-selection options. These cover activities from planning and order booking through production monitoring and shipment reporting.

## 2. Project Purpose

Garment manufacturing operations involve multiple departments and activities. Information about capacity, samples, orders, production progress, and shipment commitments needs to be available to the relevant teams.

GarmentsPro was developed to provide a desktop-based working environment for maintaining and reviewing information across several of these business areas. Its reporting facilities provide different views of operational information, including buyer-wise, order-wise, style-wise, date-range, month-wise, season-wise, department-wise, floor-wise, and line-wise reports.

## 3. Project Objectives

The documented objectives and business purposes of the application include:

- Supporting factory capacity planning and capacity review.
- Maintaining and reviewing sample planning status.
- Providing export order booking information in different business views.
- Supporting order allocation planning by date range, floor, and buyer.
- Providing order-wise visibility into production-related activities.
- Supporting sewing and cutting status monitoring.
- Providing shipment information and shipment summary reports.
- Presenting operational information through management reports, dashboards, and production charts.

## 4. Business Scope

Based on the supplied project presentation, the application covers the following business areas.

### 4.1 Capacity Planning and Review

Capacity-related reporting includes monthly capacity with ship-date range and department views, weekly capacity views, buyer summaries, booking reports, monthly booking and order information, a dashboard, and a sewing production chart.

### 4.2 Sample Planning

Sample planning reports provide status views by buyer, order number, style, sample type, merchandiser, cut plan, sewing start and finish dates, print or embroidery sent date, and selected date ranges. The presentation also lists sample plan history and sample deadline and sending-status reports.

### 4.3 Export Order Booking

Export order booking reports provide views by booked month, season, buyer, ship-date range, merchandiser, style, and department. Some listed reports include ship-date sorting or location information.

### 4.4 Order Allocation Planning

The presentation lists order allocation plan status reports by date range and floor, and by date range and buyer.

### 4.5 Production and Order Follow-up

Order-wise reporting covers production status and several related process areas, including yarn issue, fabric requirement, knitting plan and production, collar and cuff production, dyeing plan and production, laboratory test status, dyeing delivery, accessories, CAD, sewing-line production, and cutting production by order and size.

### 4.6 Sewing and Cutting Status

The presentation includes sewing status and cut-to-slip reports by date and floor, month and year, order number, and line number.

### 4.7 Shipment Information

Shipment reporting includes buyer and season, buyer/season/department, ship-date range and buyer, shipment summary status, and shipment details.

### 4.8 MIS and Management Reporting

The presentation demonstrates multiple capacity, booking, buyer-summary, dashboard, and sewing-production reporting views.
