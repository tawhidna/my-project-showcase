# GarmentsPro – Project Overview

## 1. Introduction

GarmentsPro is a desktop-based business management application developed using Microsoft Visual Basic 6.0 for the garment manufacturing industry.

The application is designed to support the operational activities of a garment manufacturing business, covering key processes from production capacity planning and order booking to order follow-up and final shipment.

It provides an integrated working environment for maintaining operational information, monitoring business activities and generating Management Information System (MIS) reports.

## 2. Project Background

Garment manufacturing involves several interconnected business processes, including capacity allocation, merchandising, order management, production follow-up and shipment coordination.

Managing these activities requires accurate information, consistent record-keeping and timely reporting.

GarmentsPro was developed to address these operational requirements through a centralized desktop application, helping users manage business information and monitor different stages of the garment order lifecycle.

## 3. Project Objectives

The primary objectives of GarmentsPro are:

- To manage garment manufacturing business operations through a structured application.
- To support capacity planning and order booking activities.
- To maintain buyer and order-related information.
- To monitor order progress throughout the operational lifecycle.
- To support production and shipment-related activities.
- To provide management with relevant operational information through MIS reports.
- To improve information accessibility and business reporting.

## 4. Business Scope

The application covers the following major business areas:

### 4.1 Capacity Planning
Supports manufacturing capacity planning and order allocation activities.

### 4.2 Merchandising
Supports merchandising-related business information and activities.

### 4.3 Order Management
Maintains order information and supports order follow-up.

### 4.4 Production Operations
Supports the monitoring of production-related business activities.

### 4.5 Inventory Management
Supports inventory-related information and activities, where applicable.

### 4.6 Commercial Operations
Supports commercial and export-related operational information, where applicable.

### 4.7 Shipment Management
Supports shipment-related information and monitoring through the final shipment stage.

### 4.8 Management Information System (MIS)
Provides operational and management reports to support business monitoring and decision-making.

## 5. Operational Workflow

GarmentsPro addresses the garment order lifecycle through the following business stages:

1. Capacity Planning
2. Order Booking
3. Merchandising and Order Management
4. Production Follow-up
5. Commercial and Shipment-related Operations
6. Shipment Completion
7. MIS Reporting and Business Analysis

The exact workflow and dependencies between these stages are documented separately in the Business Workflow documentation.
