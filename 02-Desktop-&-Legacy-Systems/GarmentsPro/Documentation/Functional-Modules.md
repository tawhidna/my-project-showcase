# GarmentsPro – Functional Modules

## 1. Purpose of This Document

This document describes the functional areas and reporting capabilities demonstrated in the GarmentsPro project presentation. It is intended to explain the business purpose of each area and help readers understand how the application supports garment manufacturing operations.

The descriptions below are based on the screens and report titles shown in the supplied presentation. A report title is treated as evidence that the corresponding report option is presented; detailed calculations, validations, approval steps, and data fields should be confirmed in the running application.

## 2. Capacity Planning and Management Reporting

### 2.1 Module Overview

The capacity planning area provides capacity-related views for reviewing planned capacity and order bookings. The presentation includes monthly and weekly capacity reports, buyer summaries, booking reports, a dashboard, and a sewing production chart.

### 2.2 Demonstrated Functions and Reports

- Month-wise capacity with ship-date range and department-wise view
- Weekly capacity for H&M brands
- Weekly capacity for all brands
- Weekly capacity for H&M brands with country-wise breakdown
- Buyer summary for H&M and brands
- Buyer summary for all buyers
- Buyer summary for premium brands
- Buyer summary for multiple brands
- Buyer summary for H&M (Rubel & Tanjir)
- Buyer summary by merchandiser head
- Buyer and product-wise summary for H&M buyers
- Merchandiser head, buyer, and product-wise summary
- Booking report
- Monthly booking and order report
- Dashboard
- Sewing production chart

### 2.3 Business Use

These views support review of capacity and booking information across different time periods and business groupings. They can help relevant users examine buyer-wise or brand-wise summaries and review booking and order information.

GarmentsPro includes a range of management-oriented reporting options. The presentation demonstrates capacity summaries, booking and order reports, buyer summaries, a dashboard, and a sewing production chart.
