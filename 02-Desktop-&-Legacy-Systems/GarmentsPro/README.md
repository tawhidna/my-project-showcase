# GarmentsPro
### Garment Manufacturing Management & MIS System

**A Visual Basic 6.0 Based Business Management Application for the Garment Manufacturing Industry**

---

## Project Overview

GarmentsPro is a garment manufacturing management application developed using Visual Basic 6.0. It is designed to manage and monitor the operational activities of a garment manufacturing business, covering the process from capacity planning and order receipt through production-related operations to final shipment.

The application brings different operational activities and management information into a centralized working environment, helping users maintain business records, monitor order progress and generate management reports.

The system also includes a collection of MIS reports to support operational monitoring, business analysis and management decision-making.

## Key Business Areas

The application covers the following business processes:

- **Capacity Planning:** Capacity allocation and order planning.
- **Order Management:** Order-related information and follow-up.
- **Production Operations:** Monitoring of production-related activities.
- **Shipment Management:** Tracking activities through the shipment stage.
- **Management Information System (MIS):** Operational reports and management-level information.

*The detailed functions of each area are documented in the respective project documentation.*
